# AVENIK Master Feature Inventory

## 1. Public Website & Core Pages
*   **Landing / Home**: Entrepreneurial intelligence gateway.
*   **About Avenik**: Platform vision and mission.
*   **Guide / How it Works**: Execution-first guide for entrepreneurs.
*   **Login & Register**: Secure authentication via NextAuth.

## 2. Entrepreneur Dashboard (The Operating System)
*   **Command Center**: The primary hub integrating notifications, early warnings, and top-level business health.
*   **Financial Intelligence**: Cash flow tracking, burn rate deterministic calculators, and capital gap analysis.
*   **Government Schemes & Support**: Hybrid semantic search for relevant government schemes based on the verified business profile (Track 1 implementation).
*   **Next-Best-Action (NBA)**: AI-driven recommendations across finance, compliance, and growth.
*   **Business Intelligence & Analytics**: Operational and historical trend analysis.
*   **Ecosystem & Partners**: Relationship mapping between businesses, investors, and suppliers.
*   **Risk & Early Warnings**: Proactive detection of compliance, financial, and operational risks.
*   **Documents & Knowledge**: Secure storage for verified identity and financial documentation.
*   **Ideas & Product Management**: Tracking entrepreneurial hypotheses through execution.
*   **Customer & Sales Intelligence**: CRM-style lifecycle tracking (Placeholder routes present).
*   **Workforce & Human Capital**: Team capacity and capability gap analysis.
*   **Legal & Governance**: Tracking compliance and IP.

## 3. Advanced Modules (Phased Rollout)
*   `advanced-funding-readiness`

## 4. Current Status
Over 80 domain-specific routes are scaffolded in the frontend. Core domains (Authentication, Government Schemes, Financial Data logic) have strong backend implementations, while many advanced domains are currently UI placeholders awaiting full AI/Backend integration.
